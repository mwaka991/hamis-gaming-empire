How To Fix PES 2025 eFootball PPSSPP Game Crashed 

If the game displays blue screen just like in the image below, showing “Game Crashed”, follow steps below on how to fix it below.

Steps to fix game crashed in PES 2025 PPSSPP are:

  1. Go to settings tab in PPSSPP Emulator.

  3. Scroll down and look for Fast Memory, untick or disable it.
  4. Also untick or disable Ignore bad memory accesses.
